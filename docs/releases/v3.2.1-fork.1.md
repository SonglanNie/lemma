The first release of this fork, based on [Lemma 3.2.1](https://github.com/fus3r/lemma/releases/tag/v3.2.1).

## Changes from Lemma 3.2.1

- **Gap between pages.** Pages are 8 pt apart by default, and Settings › Reading › Gap between pages sets the gap from 0 to 32 pt. Open books follow at once.
- **Right panel.** The notebook panel has a close button.
- **Reference previews.** The floating preview can be dragged off its line, opens at the page's width, and no longer selects the referenced text.
- **Go to.** Following a reference keeps your zoom and horizontal position. The box flashed around an equation fits the equation and its label, and a link to an equation with lettered parts, such as (15) for (15a)–(15c), goes to all of them.
- **Reference counts.** A citation written directly after a word, such as "and(24a)", is now counted.
- **Find.** ⌘F puts the cursor in the find field even when the page-number field had it.
- **Dock.** Lemma keeps one Dock icon, instead of leaving a second one among the recent apps after it is quit and opened again.
- **Updates.** Lemma no longer installs updates of the original app over this one. Check for Updates… reports new releases of this fork.

Lemma reads each book again once after this update, because equation regions and reference counts are computed differently.

## Install

Download `Lemma-3.2.1-fork.1.dmg` below, open it and drag Lemma into Applications. It runs on macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. It replaces the original Lemma if you have it, and keeps your library and settings.

This build is not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify it. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Lemma. You only do this once.
