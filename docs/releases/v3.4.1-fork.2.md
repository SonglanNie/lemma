This fork's release by @SonglanNie, based on [Lemma 3.4.1](https://github.com/fus3r/lemma/releases/tag/v3.4.1), which includes this fork's find box that keeps each book's query and its chevrons in the Classic interface, and on Lemma's change since: the confirmation for removing a library shows above Library Sources. Since 3.4.1-fork.1, ← and → turn the page.

## Changes from Lemma 3.4.1

- **Open to the Side.** Keep any part of a page in view beside the one you are reading: a figure, a table or a passage, even one a page above. ⌘-drag a box around it, or use the small arrow beside the split editor button: **This Page** (⌘\\), **Selection** and **Draw a Box…**. The side view opens fitted to the box, with the rest of the page around it, and names its tab after the figure, table or equation inside. ⌘-click still looks a word up.
- **Floating boxes.** ⇧⌘-drag a box around anything on the page, in the main editor or the side editor, or click the picture-in-picture button beside the split editor button, and it opens in a small window of its own over the place it came from, at the same size and zoom. The window holds only the box: resizing it scales the box, which stays sharp, and pinch, ⌘-scroll, ⌘+ and ⌘− zoom within it without ever reaching past its edges. Keep several, from several books: they float above every Lemma window, stay open when you switch tabs or close their book, and come back when Lemma is opened again. The button's arrow has Hide All / Show All and Close All.
- **Turning pages.** ← and → turn the page, as in PDFgear: at any zoom, in the main editor and the side editor, two pages at a time in a two-page layout. The next page's top comes to the top of the window, scrolled as far across as before. They used to do nothing on a slide that fit the window. An opened book now takes the keyboard when nothing else has it, so the arrows work without a click on the page first.
- **Peeks.** In the Classic interface, a peek dragged away from its reference now moves by its title bar every time you drag it. It used to stay where the first drag left it.
- **Zoom.** Hold ⌘ and scroll to zoom with a mouse, in the main editor, the side editor, peeks and floating boxes: scrolling up the page zooms in, and, as in PDFgear, each notch zooms by the same 19% however fast the wheel turns, so the free-spinning wheel of an MX Master goes from 10% to 10000% in under a second. Each step is drawn sharp, the point under the pointer stays where it is, and line numbers and reference counts keep their size. Opening or closing the side editor, a notebook or a derivation, changing the layout or resizing a side bar no longer changes the page's zoom. A page opened to the side starts fitted to the width of its text rather than its paper.
- **Windows.** Lemma can have several windows, each with its own tabs, panels and layout (File › New Window, ⇧⌘N). A new window takes the side bars, panel and sizes of the window it comes from. Drag a book's tab out of its window to open it in a new window there, onto another window's tabs or page to move it into that window, or choose Move Tab to New Window from the tab's menu. A book is open in one window at a time: opening it from another window brings its window to the front.
- **Copies of a PDF.** Two files with the same contents under different names, such as a contract and the copy you signed, open as two books in tabs of their own, each with its own notes and ink, and the copy is named after its file. Lemma used to open the second as the first, in its tab, and showed one copy's ink on the other. A PDF that is moved or renamed, or whose contents change on disk, still keeps its notes.
- **Updates.** Lemma no longer installs updates of the original app over this one. Check for Updates… reports new releases of this fork.

## Install

Download `Lemma-3.4.1-fork.2.dmg` (or `Lemma.dmg`) below, open it and drag Lemma into Applications. It runs on macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. It replaces the original Lemma if you have it, and keeps your library and settings; coming from the original Lemma, the tabs you had open are not reopened the first time.

This build is not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify it. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Lemma. You only do this once.
