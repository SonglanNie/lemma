This fork's release by @SonglanNie, based on [Lemma 3.3.0](https://github.com/fus3r/lemma/releases/tag/v3.3.0), which includes the reading changes and the Dock fix of v3.2.1-fork.1.

## Changes from Lemma 3.3.0

- **Open to the Side.** Keep any part of a page in view beside the one you are reading: a figure, a table or a passage, even one a page above. ⌘-drag a box around it, or use the small arrow beside the split editor button: **This Page** (⌘\\), **Selection** and **Draw a Box…**. The side view opens fitted to the box, with the rest of the page around it, and names its tab after the figure, table or equation inside. ⌘-click still looks a word up.
- **Floating boxes.** ⇧⌘-drag a box around anything on the page, in the main editor or the side editor, or click the picture-in-picture button beside the split editor button, and it opens in a small window of its own over the place it came from, at the same size and zoom. The window holds only the box: resizing it scales the box, which stays sharp, and pinch, ⌘+ and ⌘− zoom within it without ever reaching past its edges. Keep several, from several books: they float above every Lemma window, stay open when you switch tabs or close their book, and come back when Lemma is opened again. The button's arrow has Hide All / Show All and Close All.
- **Zoom.** Opening or closing the side editor, a notebook or a derivation, changing the layout or resizing a side bar no longer changes the page's zoom. A page opened to the side starts fitted to the width of its text rather than its paper.
- **Reference previews** open with the page at a little less than your reading zoom, as wide as that page, so a page you read zoomed in is no longer shown at a fraction of its size. Resize the preview by its right edge, bottom edge or corner, as you would a window (by its top edge and top-right corner when it opens above the reference); the page refits to the new width. They stay on screen beside the reference they point at: near the edge of a wide window, the preview used to grow off the screen, cutting off its right side and its buttons.
- **Windows.** Lemma can have several windows, each with its own tabs, panels and layout (File › New Window, ⇧⌘N). A new window takes the side bars, panel and sizes of the window it comes from. Drag a book's tab out of its window to open it in a new window there, onto another window's tabs or page to move it into that window, or choose Move Tab to New Window from the tab's menu. A book is open in one window at a time: opening it from another window brings its window to the front. Tabs can now be dragged to the right along the tab bar too.
- **Updates.** Lemma no longer installs updates of the original app over this one. Check for Updates… reports new releases of this fork.

## Install

Download `Lemma-3.3.0-fork.1.dmg` below, open it and drag Lemma into Applications. It runs on macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. It replaces the original Lemma if you have it, and keeps your library and settings; coming from the original Lemma, the tabs you had open are not reopened the first time.

This build is not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify it. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Lemma. You only do this once.
