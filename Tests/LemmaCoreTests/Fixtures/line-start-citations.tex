\begin{subequations}
\begin{align}
\Delta Y_0 &= \Delta g_0 + h \Delta g_1, \label{a}\\
\Delta g_0 &= \mathcal{O}(1), \label{b}\\

\end{align}
\end{subequations}

The square bracket is $\Delta Y_0$, and we now prove the bound for $i = r$. In\linebreak
\eqref{b} for $\Delta g_0^{\{r-1\}}$ we have $\ell + k \le r - 1$ and $m_j \le r - 1$, so $\Delta g_0 = \mathcal{O}(1)$, and hence\linebreak
\eqref{a}, so $y_{n+1} = Y_{n,s}$ with $b_1 = 1$ also $c_s = 1$, as \eqref{a} and \eqref{b} require.
