Opening Lemma the first time
============================

This copy of Lemma is free and open source, but it is not yet notarized by Apple (that needs a paid
developer membership). The first time you open it, macOS says it "cannot verify" Lemma. To open it anyway:

1. Drag Lemma into Applications, then double-click it once. macOS shows its warning: click Done.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Lemma was blocked to protect your Mac",
   and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
3. Click Open in the last dialog. From then on Lemma opens like any other app.

Or, in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lemma.app

You can check the source and build Lemma yourself: https://github.com/SonglanNie/lemma
